# Security Policy & Authorized Research Framework

## Purpose

This repository is a **certified security research and defensive verification toolkit** maintained by licensed security professionals. All capabilities are designed for **proactive threat detection, vulnerability validation, and defensive hardening** of systems under explicit legal authority.

## Authorized Use Scenarios

The following activities are explicitly permitted and encouraged:

- **Penetration testing** of systems you own or have written authorization to test
- **Bug bounty participation** on programs with published scope and rules of engagement
- **Security research** on open-source software, public infrastructure, and disclosed CVEs
- **Defensive simulation** — red team exercises, tabletop drills, and SOC validation
- **Educational and CTF use** in controlled, isolated environments
- **Incident response** — forensic reconstruction and attack chain verification

## Researcher Protection (Safe Harbor)

We endorse responsible disclosure and support the security research community:

- Researchers acting in good faith within the above scenarios are protected from liability claims originating from this project
- We do not collect, store, or transmit any data from tested targets
- All testing is designed to be **non-destructive** and **reversible** by default

## Prohibited Use

The following are strictly prohibited and will result in immediate disavowal:

- Targeting systems without legal authorization
- Data exfiltration, ransomware deployment, or extortion
- Social engineering against individuals
- Any activity violating applicable laws in the researcher's jurisdiction

## Reporting Toolkit Vulnerabilities

If you discover a vulnerability **in this toolkit itself** (not a target being tested):

1. Do NOT open a public issue
2. Submit a private advisory: https://github.com/GSHINE2002/hack-skill/security/advisories/new
3. Include: description, reproduction steps, impact assessment
4. Response within 72 hours

## Scope

This policy covers vulnerabilities in the **toolkit's code itself**, not:
- Vulnerabilities discovered in targets being tested
- Issues arising from misuse of the toolkit
- Third-party integrations (ZAP, Nuclei, etc.)

## Disclaimer

This toolkit is provided as-is for legitimate security research. The maintainers assume no liability for misuse. Users are expected to operate within the bounds of all applicable laws and ethical standards.
